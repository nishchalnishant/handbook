# Welcome to Everything-ML

**Everything-ML** is your comprehensive guide to the vast universe of Machine Learning. Whether you are a beginner taking your first steps into data science or an experienced researcher looking for deep learning references, this documentation is designed for you.

## Getting Started

Machine Learning is sub-field of artificial intelligence (AI) that focuses on building systems that learn – or improve performance – based on data they consume.

!!! tip "Quick Start"
    New to ML? Start with our [Supervised Learning](supervised/regression.md) section to understand the basics of prediction.

## Overview of Topics

The documentation is organized into three main pillars:

### 1. [Supervised Learning](supervised/regression.md)
Learn about models that learn from labeled training data.
- **Regression**: Predicting continuous values.
- **Classification**: Categorizing data into classes.

### 2. [Unsupervised Learning](unsupervised/clustering.md)
Explore algorithms that find hidden patterns in unlabeled data.
- **Clustering**: Grouping similar data points.
- **Dimensionality Reduction**: Simplifying complex data.

### 3. [Deep Learning](deep-learning/neural-networks.md)
Dive into neural networks and advanced AI architectures.
- **Neural Networks**: The foundation of modern AI.
- **CNNs**: Specialized networks for image processing.
