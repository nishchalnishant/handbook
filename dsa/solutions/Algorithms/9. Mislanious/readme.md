# Miscellaneous Algorithms Overview 🧩

A collection of algorithms that don't fit perfectly into other categories or combine multiple concepts.

## Problems & Solutions

### Easy
1. **[Roman to Integer](Easy/roman_to_int.py)**
   - Convert Roman numerals to integer.
2. **[Pascal's Triangle](Easy/pascals_triangle.py)**
   - Generate rows of Pascal's triangle.

### Medium
1. **[Integer to Roman](Medium/int_to_roman.py)**
   - Convert integer to Roman numerals.
2. **[Spiral Matrix](Medium/spiral_matrix.py)**
   - Traverse matrix in spiral order.
